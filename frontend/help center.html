Returns & Exchanges

Our policy on sending pieces back

You can return most pieces within 3 days of delivery, as long as they're unworn, undamaged, and in their original gift box with tags attached.

For hygiene reasons, pierced earrings can only be returned if they arrive faulty. Custom or personalised pieces (engraved or made to a specific size) are also final sale unless there's a defect.

Once we've received and checked the returned piece, we send your refund straight to the M‑Pesa number used for the order. This usually takes 1–3 business days after inspection.

Product Care & Materials

Keeping your jewelry looking new

Xuping pieces are gold‑plated over a brass or alloy base a lightweight, affordable way to get a solid‑gold look. Stainless steel and pearl pieces are listed separately in each product's description so you always know exactly what you're getting.

  • Gold‑plated (Xuping) & brass: keep away from water, perfume and lotion; store in the pouch provided.
  • Stainless steel: low maintenance wipe with a soft, dry cloth.
  • Pearl: avoid moisture and spray perfume before putting pieces on, not after; store flat, wrapped in soft cloth.
  • Leather (belts & watches): keep dry and away from direct sun; condition occasionally.
