About Us

Your jewelry store in Ruaka.

Get to know the store, what we stand for, and the people behind Sj Gems Jewelry Store.

Since 2024

Jewelry and accessories for everyday and special moments.

Jewelry has adorned people for thousands of years worn as a mark of status, a keepsake of love, or a quiet piece of personal history passed from one hand to the next.

From hammered gold cuffs to hand-strung pearls, every era has shaped precious metal and stone into something meant to be kept, not just worn.

At Sj Gems Jewelry Store in Ruaka we stock necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets, jewelry sets, watches, belts, eyewear and gift accessories, including Xuping, stainless steel, brass, pearl and leather pieces. New stock arrives every week, so the collection keeps changing.

Our Vision

To be Ruaka's trusted home for jewelry a place where every client, near or far, can find a piece that feels made for them.

Our Mission

We bring a wide choice of jewelry and accessories to clients in Ruaka and across Kenya, with honest pricing and personal service from the first visit, or the first M-Pesa order, to the piece you take home.
