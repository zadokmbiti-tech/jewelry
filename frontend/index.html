Since 2024

Cast in small batches, finished entirely by hand.

Jewelry has adorned people for thousands of years worn as a mark of status, a keepsake of love, or a quiet piece of personal history passed from one hand to the next.

From hammered gold cuffs to hand-strung pearls, every era has shaped precious metal and stone into something meant to be kept, not just worn.

Every Sj Gems Jewelry Store piece starts as a wax carving on a workbench in Ruaka and from different parts of the globe. We cast in recycled gold and sterling silver, then stone-set and polish each piece ourselves no two trays ever look quite the same.

Our Vision

To be Ruaka's trusted home for jewelry a place where every client, near or far, can find a piece that feels made for them.

Our Mission

We connect our workshop directly to our clients, offering honest pricing, genuine materials, and personal service from the first visit to the piece you take home.
